# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- Instagram downloads: paste an Instagram reel or post link in "+ From YouTube / Instagram".
- New "Use my browser login" option for sites that need you logged in (Instagram usually does). Firefox works best.
- "Use a cookies file…" for when reading the browser login doesn't work. If you already saved a cookies file for Shorts Uploader, it's used automatically.

## 1.3.0
- New: "+ From YouTube" button. Paste a YouTube link (TikTok, SoundCloud and many other sites work too) and its audio is downloaded and added as a new sound automatically.
- The first time, it downloads the downloader (about 80 MB). It keeps itself updated, because YouTube changes often.
- Videos up to 15 minutes. Use Trim afterwards to cut it down to the part you want.
- Removing a downloaded sound also deletes its file, so it doesn't take up space.

## 1.2.0
- New: Trim sounds. Right-click a sound and choose Trim to cut off a long intro or ending. Drag the start and end lines on the waveform, and press Preview to hear it in your headphones only. Your original file isn't changed. Trimmed sounds show a ✂ on their button.
- Removing a sound now asks first, and a hint under the buttons shows what right-click can do.

## 1.1.0
- New: Noise suppression for your mic. Tick it under Volume to remove keyboard clicks, mouse clicks and background noise. Only your voice is filtered, so your sounds play normally.
- The first time you turn it on, it downloads a small filter (about 13 MB).
- If you were using AMD Noise Suppression, you can turn that off and set "Your microphone" back to your real mic.

## 1.0.0
- First version as an installable app: Start Menu and desktop icons, and automatic updates from GitHub.
- Play sounds through your mic into Discord, with global hotkeys, separate volumes for the call and your ears, and a "Stop all" button.
- Help menu: check for updates, what's new, open the app folder or log file.
